F3-tools

Plays when: you press any of the eight keys in the FN tray.

Drop your sound here (.mp3, .wav or .m4a).
RP Sound Push renames it and sends it to the phone by itself.
(Without RP Sound Push: name it sound.mp3 yourself, then push.)

This is the one sound for all of them. To give one key its own sound,
use its folder instead: F4-tool-srch, F5-tool-log, F6-tool-hlth, F7-tool-lgnd,
F8-tool-cal, F9-tool-ref, F10-tool-vis, F11-tool-prfl.

A tray key's sound covers its whole move: the tray stows and the drum turns
without their own sounds, so you hear just this one.

No file here: the key click (K1-key).

Hear it: MENU › SOUND TEST on the RP.
