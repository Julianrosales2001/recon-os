K2-jog

Plays when: the jog dial clicks.

original.wav is the RP's own sound. Keep it.
To replace it, drop yours in next to it, named exactly:

  sound.mp3

sound.wav and sound.m4a work too. Yours plays instead of the original;
delete yours and the original comes back.

When these folders are empty they play this one too:
  R1-ring-pick, T3-train-down

Hear it: MENU › SOUND TEST on the RP.
